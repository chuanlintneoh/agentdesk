  > - 2022: $170,782,000,000 / $394,328,000,000 = 43.3%
  >
  > 2. **Operating Margin**: This indicates the efficiency of the company's operations.
  >
  > - 2025: $133,050,000,000 / $416,161,000,000 = 32.0%
  > - 2024: $123,216,000,000 / $391,035,000,000 = 31.5%
  > - 2023: $114,301,000,000 / $383,285,000,000 = 29.8%
  > - 2022: $119,437,000,000 / $394,328,000,000 = 30.3%

  > 3. **Net Profit Margin**: This shows the percentage of revenue that remains as net income.

  > - 2025: $112,010,000,000 / $416,161,000,000 = 26.9%
  > - 2024: $93,736,000,000 / $391,035,000,000 = 24.0%

  > - 2022: $99,803,000,000 / $394,328,000,000 = 25.3%

  > The qualitative operational narrative in the internal corporate text records seems to align with these empirical trends. The narrative also emphasizes the company's focus on financial condition, results of operations, and cash flows, which aligns with the calculated financial margins.

  > However, without a more detailed analysis of the text records, it's challenging to provide a comprehensive evaluation of the alignment between the qualitative narrative and the empirical trends. The narrative pieces provided suggest a cautious approach to future operations and investments, which could be reflected in the company's financial performance and margins.
